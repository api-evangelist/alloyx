MANAMA, Bahrain / NEW YORK — September 29, 2026 — AX COIN BAHRAIN B.S.C. Closed ("AX Coin"), the regulated stablecoin issuer of Solowin Holdings (Nasdaq: AXG), today announced that it has entered into a Memorandum of Understanding (the "MOU") with Codex Financial Inc. ("Codex"), a stablecoin-native FX platform serving institutional and enterprise clients.
